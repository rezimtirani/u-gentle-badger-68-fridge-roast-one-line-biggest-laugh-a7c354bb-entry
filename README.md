One single-line roast of a smart fridge: twenty minutes of firmware updating, then a proud announcement that the milk expired — days after the milk left and took the eggs with it.

> My smart fridge spent twenty minutes updating its firmware, then proudly announced the milk expired — the milk left on Tuesday and took the eggs with it.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3 standard library only) writes the roast to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`, checked first against the competition gate: 1 to 200 characters of UTF-8 text, a single line.

```sh
python3 main.py
```
